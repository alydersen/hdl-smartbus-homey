Connect your HDL SmartBus installation to Homey and give it the logic it deserves.

HDL SmartBus for Homey supports the most common HDL device types, so you can control and monitor your existing installation directly from Homey or from flows:

- Relays and dimmers, per channel
- Multisensors (motion, temperature, humidity, luminance, dry contacts)
- Standalone dry contact modules
- Floor heating controllers
- Curtain motors
- DLP panels (as temperature sensors)
- Universal switches for triggering flows from HDL buttons and logic

You can control your devices from the Homey app, use them in flows, and build automations on top of your wired HDL installation.

REQUIREMENTS

You need an HDL IP-gateway connected to your HDL bus, on the same network as your Homey. Devices on the bus are discovered automatically when you pair them.
