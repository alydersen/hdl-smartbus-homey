HDL Smartbus settings

For the app to communicate with the smartbus, the first thing needed is the IP-address of the IP-module. The subnet is your HDL bus subnet, and then the ID of this app in your subnet. It is important that this ID is available (not taken by another unit on the bus).

The app polls all devices for their state at a regular interval. The default is every 60 seconds. A shorter interval makes Homey notice state changes faster, but puts more traffic on the HDL bus.

Motion Sensor Universal Switch

To catch motion and no motion from a motion sensor, you need create logic that sends a universal switch broadcast with true if motion and false if no motion. The default universal switch number is 212. Change it here if you need to.

Universal Switches

When you change a Universal Switch in the app, the change is always sent as a broadcast on the HDL bus. In addition to the broadcast, the app sends the change according to the send mode below. The legacy mode is the behavior of earlier app versions and is kept as the default; if your installation works with broadcasts alone, switching to "Broadcast only" puts far less traffic on the HDL bus.

If you set the ID of a logic controller here, a copy of any change on a Universal Switch from this Homey app is sent to that controller in addition to the broadcast (for the send modes that include the logic controller).

Universal Switch Troubleshooting

Enable this to record the last 100 universal switch signals the app received on the bus, including signals that are normally filtered out. This is useful if switches appear to change on their own. Keep it off when you do not need it.
